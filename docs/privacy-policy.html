Sil-More Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Sil-More game for Android, published by the Sil-More project.

Data collection and sharing

Sil-More does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, gameplay data, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, or other user or device data with the developer or third parties. The game does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or user-account services.

Data stored on your device

Sil-More stores game saves, character names, gameplay and run history, preferences, and diagnostic logs in the app's private storage on your device. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not accessible to the Sil-More developer.

Data security

Sil-More relies on Android's app-private storage protections for its local data. Because the game does not transmit user data, it does not send that data over a network connection.

Android backup

If Android backup is enabled for your device, Android may back up and restore Sil-More's app data through the backup service associated with your device or Google account. This is an operating-system service: Sil-More does not operate the backup service and the Sil-More developer cannot access your backups. The backup provider's privacy policy and your device backup settings govern that service.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear Sil-More's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Backup copies are retained and deleted according to your Android or Google account backup settings and the backup provider's policies.

Children's privacy

Sil-More does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Sil-More's data practices change, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the changed version is distributed.

Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted to the Sil-More project through its public issue tracker. Do not include private or sensitive information in a public issue.
